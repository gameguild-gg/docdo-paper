Abdominal vascular segmentation from CT remains the bottleneck for image-guided surgery: missed branches and broken centerlines directly impact renal and hepatic resection planning.

3D preoperative planning reduces positive surgical margins in partial nephrectomy by \alert{\textbf{23--47\,\%}}.\footnote{\scriptsize Porpiglia et al., \emph{BJU Int.} 121(4):574--581, 2018.} A survey of $N=387$ surgeons shows \alert{\textbf{68\,\%}} cite ``time-intensive preparation'' as the primary adoption barrier.\footnote{\scriptsize Simpfend\"orfer et al., \emph{J.~Robotic Surg.} 10(4):321--329, 2016.}

\begin{frame}{Seeded Image Foresting Transform (IFT)}
\footnotesize
\textit{Fundamental principle: Each voxel is assigned the label of the seed whose minimal-cost path reaches it.}

\vspace{0.15cm}
\begin{columns}[T]
\begin{column}{0.48\linewidth}
  \begin{block}{Algorithm flow}
    \scriptsize
    \begin{enumerate}
    \setlength\itemsep{1pt}
    \item \textbf{Initialize:} Seeds in priority queue; $D(s)=0$, others $\infty$.
    \item \textbf{Pop:} Extract voxel $u$ with minimal cost $D(u)$.
    \item \textbf{Check limits:} Halt branch if $D(u) > B$ or $|I(u)-\mu_S| > T$.
    \item \textbf{Relax:} For each neighbor $v$, if $D(u) + w(u,v) < D(v)$:
      Update $D(v)$ and push/update in queue.
    \item \textbf{Loop:} Repeat until queue is empty.
    \end{enumerate}
  \end{block}
\end{column}
\begin{column}{0.48\linewidth}
  \begin{block}{DocDo Clinical Adaptation}
    \begin{itemize}
    \setlength\itemsep{4pt}
    \item \textbf{Search Model:} Multi-source Dijkstra on a priority queue.
    \item \textbf{Path Cost:} \emph{Additive} --- sum of intensity differences $|I(u)-I(v)|$.
    \item \textbf{Algorithmic Pruning:} Engineered \alert{budgets}, \alert{thresholds}, and \alert{iteration ceilings}.
    \item \textbf{Result:} Deterministic, auditable, \textbf{surgeon-controllable}.
    \end{itemize}
  \end{block}
\end{column}
\end{columns}
\vspace{0.1cm}
{\scriptsize\color{gray} Path cost comparison: Classical IFT uses bottleneck cost $\max_{e\in\pi} w(e)$ (Falc\~ao 2004); DocDo uses additive cost $\sum_{e\in\pi} w(e)$ with $w(u,v) = |I(u)-I(v)|$ to control leakages.}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Orthogonal Vessel Tracker and failure modes}
\footnotesize
\begin{columns}[T]
\begin{column}{0.54\linewidth}
  \begin{block}{OVT iterative ridge tracking}
    \scriptsize
    \begin{enumerate}
    \setlength\itemsep{1pt}
    \item Compute Hessian in orthogonal plane to propagation
    \item Dominant eigenvector gives orientation \& radius
    \item Fit a Gaussian cross-section model
    \item Advance seed along the ridge
    \end{enumerate}
  \end{block}
\end{column}
\begin{column}{0.42\linewidth}
  \begin{block}{Clinical Auditability Properties}
    \scriptsize
    \begin{itemize}
    \setlength\itemsep{1pt}
    \item Geometrically explicit centerlines
    \item Local radius estimation at every point
    \item Every step inspectable and auditable
    \end{itemize}
  \end{block}
\end{column}
\end{columns}
\vspace{0.1cm}
\begin{columns}[T]
\begin{column}{0.48\linewidth}
  \begin{block}{Bifurcations}
    \scriptsize
    Single-ridge model breaks at splits; Hessian eigenstructure becomes ambiguous, tracker stalls or oscillates.
  \end{block}
\end{column}
\begin{column}{0.48\linewidth}
  \begin{block}{Non-circular cross-sections}
    \scriptsize
    Renal/portal veins are elliptical or tortuous; Gaussian fit diverges, halting centerlines prematurely.
  \end{block}
\end{column}
\end{columns}
\vspace{0.05cm}
\begin{alertblock}{Clinical impact on prior DocDo work}
\scriptsize Tracker implemented but excluded from active workflow; pipeline falls back to Seeded IFT for vessels, losing topological connectivity.
\end{alertblock}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Topology- and geometry-aware learning}
\footnotesize
\begin{columns}[T]
\begin{column}{0.48\linewidth}
\begin{block}{clDice: skeleton-based loss}
\scriptsize
Augments standard Dice with soft-skeleton term. Penalizes disconnected centerlines without extra inference cost. Reduces connectivity errors by \textbf{20--40\,\%} on tubular structures. Not yet applied to 3D abdominal vessels.
\end{block}
\vspace{0.1cm}
\begin{block}{Persistent homology loss}
\scriptsize
Differentiable approximation to Wasserstein distance between persistence diagrams. Enforces correct \textbf{Betti numbers}: $\beta_0$ (connected components) and $\beta_1$ (loops). Targets missed bifurcations and spurious bridges.
\end{block}
\end{column}
\begin{column}{0.48\linewidth}
\begin{block}{Branch-aware centerline loss}
\scriptsize
Penalizes missing daughter vessels at bifurcation points. Couples to the differentiable vessel tracker, which provides explicit centerline and radius estimates.
\end{block}
\vspace{0.15cm}
\begin{alertblock}{Topological Loss Synergy}
\scriptsize
Synergy: topology-aware losses + differentiable classical pipeline = topologically correct, surgeon-editable delineations with low cognitive load.
\end{alertblock}
\end{column}
\end{columns}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Differentiable Seeded IFT: Formulation}
\footnotesize
\begin{align*}
\textbf{Softmin Cost:} \quad & \tilde{C}^\tau(s,t) = -\tau \log \sum_{\pi:s\to t} \exp\left(-\frac{1}{\tau}\sum_{e\in\pi} w(e)\right) \\[0.25em]
\textbf{Path Probability:} \quad & p(\pi|s,t) = \frac{\exp\left(-\frac{1}{\tau}\sum_{e\in\pi} w(e)\right)}{\sum_{\pi':s\to t} \exp\left(-\frac{1}{\tau}\sum_{e'\in\pi'} w(e')\right)} \\[0.25em]
\textbf{Gradient:} \quad & \frac{\partial \tilde{C}^\tau}{\partial w(e)} = \sum_{\pi\ni e} p(\pi|s,t)
\end{align*}
\vspace{-0.1cm}
\begin{alertblock}{Theoretical Foundation (Min-Bottleneck vs.\ Sum-Path)}
\scriptsize
Hard IFT $C^* = \min_\pi \max_{e\in\pi} w(e)$ is a min-bottleneck path. The soft surrogate is a softmin of sum-path costs. They coincide only when $w(e)$ is binary; otherwise the sum form is a strict relaxation chosen for gradient flow. Deployment uses the sum form with $\tau\to0$ to yield a deterministic shortest-path forest (re-definition, not recovery).
\end{alertblock}
{\scriptsize\color{gray} IFT DAG + per-voxel audit preserved; cost redefined (bottleneck $\to$ additive).}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Optimization Guarantees, Differentiable OVT, and Coupling}
\scriptsize
\begin{block}{Optimization \& Inference Mechanics}
\begin{itemize}
\setlength\itemsep{1pt}
\item \textbf{Gradient flow:} topology-aware loss back-propagates through softmin to CNN edge-weight parameters
\item \textbf{Deterministic deployment:} at inference, harden $\tau\to 0$ to recover a deterministic shortest-path forest
\item \textbf{Editability preserved:} surgeon seeds at inference still steer the path forest --- every correction $\le 2$\,s
\end{itemize}
\end{block}
\vspace{0.05cm}
\begin{block}{Differentiable Orthogonal Vessel Tracker}
\begin{itemize}
\setlength\itemsep{1pt}
\item Learnable ridge response via Hessian eigenvalue regression \textit{(predicts 3D curvature eigenvalues $\lambda_i$ to quantify vesselness/radius directly)}
\item Learnable elliptical cross-section estimator (handles non-circular vessels)
\item Differentiable step size and stopping criteria
\end{itemize}
\end{block}
\vspace{0.05cm}
\begin{alertblock}{Pipeline Integration \& Optimization Flow}
Input CT $\to$ 3D CNN/Transformer $\to$ $w(e)$ + ridge responses $\to$ Diff.\ IFT + Diff.\ OVT $\to$ topology fusion $\to$ segmentation + centerlines/radii.
\end{alertblock}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Backup --- IFT operational limits}
\footnotesize
\begin{columns}[T]
\begin{column}{0.48\linewidth}
\begin{block}{Hand-crafted costs}
\scriptsize
Edge weights are designed per organ and per contrast phase. Every new acquisition protocol requires re-tuning, making large-scale generalization impractical.
\end{block}
\vspace{0.1cm}
\begin{block}{Partial-volume sensitivity}
\scriptsize
At thin-walled structures (renal papillae, vessel walls, bile duct), the integer-valued boundary metric leaks through sub-voxel transitions.
\end{block}
\end{column}
\begin{column}{0.48\linewidth}
\begin{block}{Multi-class scaling}
\scriptsize
Concurrent labelling of 7+ anatomical classes (liver, kidney, pancreas, spleen, vasculature) requires sequential or competitive seeding strategies that scale poorly in time and memory.
\end{block}
\vspace{0.05cm}
\begin{alertblock}{Implication for the differentiable hybrid method}
\scriptsize
These three limits motivate the differentiable formulation: \alert{learn the costs end-to-end} while preserving the algorithmic pipeline surgeons depend on.
\end{alertblock}
\end{column}
\end{columns}
\vspace{0.1cm}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Backup --- Differentiable classical pipelines}
\footnotesize
\begin{columns}[T]
\begin{column}{0.48\linewidth}
\begin{block}{Softening the Seeded IFT}
\scriptsize
Hard bottleneck cost: $C^*(s,t) = \min_{\pi: s\to t} \max_{e\in\pi} w(e)$

Soft approximation: $\tilde{C}^\tau(s,t) = -\tau \log \sum_{\pi: s\to t} \exp\!\Bigl(-\tfrac{1}{\tau}\textstyle\sum_{e\in\pi} w(e)\Bigr)$

Temperature $\tau \to 0^+$ recovers $C^*(s,t)$; larger $\tau$ yields smooth gradients.
\end{block}
\vspace{0.1cm}
\begin{block}{End-to-end training}
\scriptsize
Edge weights $w(e)$ are learned by the CNN feature encoder. Back-propagation through $\tilde{C}^\tau$ trains the network to minimize topology-aware losses.
\end{block}
\end{column}
\begin{column}{0.48\linewidth}
\begin{block}{Differentiable vessel tracking}
\scriptsize
Replace argmax orientation estimate with soft-attention readout over eigenvector field. Gaussian cross-section fit becomes differentiable w.r.t. learned Hessian response.
\end{block}
\vspace{0.15cm}
\begin{alertblock}{Research Gap: Unified Differentiation}
\scriptsize
First instance of end-to-end differentiation of both Seeded IFT \emph{and} orthogonal vessel tracking on 3D abdominal CT, trained jointly with topology-aware losses.
\end{alertblock}
\end{column}
\end{columns}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Backup --- Detailed Evaluation \& Loss Metrics}
\scriptsize
\begin{columns}[T]
\begin{column}{0.49\linewidth}
\begin{block}{Topological \& Structural Metrics}
  \begin{itemize}
  \setlength\itemsep{1pt}
  \item \textbf{clDice (Centerline Dice):} Skeleton overlap on soft centerlines $S_p, S_g$: \\
    \hfil $\text{clDice} = \frac{2 \cdot T_{\text{prec}}(S_p, V_g) \cdot T_{\text{rec}}(S_g, V_p)}{T_{\text{prec}} + T_{\text{rec}}}$ \\
    Penalizes vessel interruptions without inflating volume.
  \item \textbf{Betti Numbers ($\beta_0, \beta_1$):}
    \begin{itemize}\tiny
    \item $\beta_0$: Connected components (vessel continuity).
    \item $\beta_1$: Topological loops/tunnels (spurious bridges).
    \end{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Wasserstein Loss ($W_p$):} Distance on 0D/1D persistence diagrams $D(f)$ matching birth-death topological features.
  \end{itemize}
\end{block}
\end{column}

\begin{column}{0.49\linewidth}
\begin{block}{Geometric \& Boundary Metrics}
  \begin{itemize}
  \setlength\itemsep{1pt}
  \item \textbf{Dice (DSC):} $\frac{2|A \cap B|}{|A| + |B|}$ (volumetric overlap).
  \item \textbf{HD95 (95\% Hausdorff):} 95th percentile of surface distances; robust boundary error metric.
  \item \textbf{ASSD:} Average symmetric surface distance between $\partial A$ and $\partial B$.
  \end{itemize}
\end{block}

\begin{block}{Clinical Usability Metrics}
  \begin{itemize}
  \setlength\itemsep{1pt}
  \item \textbf{NASA-TLX:} 6-dimension workload index (mental, physical, temporal demand, performance, effort, frustration).
  \item \textbf{SUS (System Usability Scale):} 10-item usability survey score (target score $> 80$).
  \end{itemize}
\end{block}
\end{column}
\end{columns}
\end{frame}
